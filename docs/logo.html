Idle animation

For the idle / no-video state.

  • The body bobs and tilts slowly.
  • The eyes glance around and blink, occasionally twice in a row.
  • The tongue bends along its length and ripples, rather than swinging from a hinge.
  • Every few seconds the tongue is slurped back in and the mouth closes into a smile; a few seconds later the mouth opens and the tongue pops back out with a wobble, and he looks down at it.
