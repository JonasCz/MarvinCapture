MarvinCapture

Open-source driver and capture software for the Pinnacle “Marvin” USB DV capture boxes (Pinnacle Studio 500-USB, 510-USB, and relatives) on Apple Silicon Macs.

This installer puts the MarvinCapture app in /Applications and, if you keep that option selected, the MarvinCaptureCLI command-line tool in /usr/local.

Requires macOS 15 or later.
